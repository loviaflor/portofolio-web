As part of the Temasek Foundation Specialists' Community Action and Leadership Exchange (TF SCALE) in Singapore, our team of eight designed SafeSpace, a community app that gives the Malay community an anonymous place to talk openly about HPV vaccination and sexual health.

## The exchange program

This was a fully funded, two-week student exchange at Singapore Polytechnic in March 2024, supported by the Temasek Foundation. The program centered on a real case provided by MSD Singapore.

## The challenge

HPV vaccination rates in Singapore are lowest within the Malay community. The case asked our team to understand why, and to propose a solution that would work for the people it was meant to serve.

## What we did

We moved through interviews, ideation, and prototyping. Listening to the community came first, so the solution could be shaped around their real concerns rather than our assumptions.

The result was SafeSpace: a community-driven app where people can anonymously share personal concerns, exchange health experiences, and access reliable information about the HPV vaccine and sexual health. By building open dialogue and trust, SafeSpace aims to raise awareness and encourage vaccination within the community.
