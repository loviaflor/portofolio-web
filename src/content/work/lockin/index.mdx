Recursion 1.0 2025, short for Red Campus Futurist Informatics Competition, is organized by the Informatics Student Faculty of Hasanuddin University. Our team developed LockIN from the current problem that our generation faces, excessive screen time and overstimulation. In this competition, our team won the Best Presentation award for achieving the 4th place out of all the 10 team finalists.


## About LockIN

LockIN addresses excessive smartphone use and digital distractions that affect productivity, focus, and healthy screen-time habits among smartphone users. Designed for people who want greater control over their digital routines, LockIN combines app access restrictions with a physical NFC keychain unlocker and customizable access settings to help users build healthier screen-time habits.
