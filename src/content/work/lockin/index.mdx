Recursion 1.0 2025, short for Red Campus Futurist Informatics Competition, is organized by the Informatics Student Faculty of Hasanuddin University. Our team developed LockIN from the current problem that our generation faces, excessive screen time and overstimulation.

## The competition

Recursion 1.0 challenges students to design technology-driven solutions. Our entry took LockIN through the full path a product follows: defining the idea, designing the experience, and presenting it to the judges.

## What LockIN is

LockIN addresses excessive smartphone use and digital distractions that affect productivity, focus, and healthy screen-time habits among smartphone users. Designed for people who want greater control over their digital routines, LockIN combines app access restrictions with a physical NFC keychain unlocker and customizable access settings to help users build healthier screen-time habits.
