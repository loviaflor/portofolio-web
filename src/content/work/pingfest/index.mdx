My team, Rubber Ducklings, won 1st place at the P!NGFEST 2025 Data Analytics Competition, an annual technology festival organized by BEM FATISDA (Badan Eksekutif Mahasiswa Fakultas Teknologi Informasi dan Sains Data) at Universitas Sebelas Maret (UNS), for a comprehensive sales and customer analytics dashboard built with SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS) and Power BI.

## Sales Performance Dashboard

Working from an e-commerce Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) containing Store, Transaction, Customer, and Product data, we carried out end-to-end data cleaning, transformation, and visualization.

The final dashboard delivered insights on sales performance, customer behavior patterns, revenue distribution by city, and online vs. offline sales dynamics, helping translate raw data into actionable business insights.
