Our team won 1st place at the BStartion Business Plan Competition 2024, held by Binus Entrepreneur, with Pinjemin, a peer-to-peer lending app built around a simple idea: most of the things we own are used only occasionally, so why not share them?

## The idea

Pinjemin lets people lend items they rarely use to neighbors and others in their city. Instead of everyone buying something they need once or twice, the item already sitting in someone's home gets a second life. By reducing production and consumption, the app aims to build a more sustainable environment.
