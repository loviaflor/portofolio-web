Built for the TNB Aura Venture Capital Firm's International Business Case Competition, our pitch deck made the case for FaceReality as a market entry investment in Indonesia's beauty sector, and earned our team a place in the National Finals.

## The challenge

TNB Aura Venture Capital Firm asked competing teams to build an investment case for a company entering a new market. Our team of four chose to position FaceReality for entry into Indonesia, within the scope of beauty.

## What we built

We developed a full pitch deck that framed FaceReality as a credible market entry opportunity and presented the reasoning to the judges as a clear, investment-ready story.

## The National Finals

Our deck advanced to the National Finals, where we competed against 6 other teams from Indonesia. As finalists, we presented the pitch deck for FaceReality's market entry into Indonesia's beauty sector, and defended our reasoning in front of the judges.

## Visiting Eratani

As finalists, we also visited Eratani, one of the companies invested in by TNB Aura Venture Capital. We met its CEO and learned about Indonesian farmers and how the business works, which gave us a closer look at how a venture capital firm backs a company beyond the pitch itself.
